## 🔬 About the Research

Smart grids are an important component of modern smart-city infrastructure because they support efficient and reliable energy distribution.

This research investigates the use of machine learning and deep learning techniques for predicting smart-grid behavior and power consumption.

The study compares multiple computational models to evaluate their effectiveness in smart-grid prediction. The results indicate that deep-learning-based approaches can provide higher prediction accuracy than traditional machine-learning methods.

Accurate prediction of power consumption and grid behavior can contribute to:

- Improved energy distribution
- Better grid stability
- More reliable power-management systems
- Efficient utilization of energy resources
- Development of intelligent smart-city infrastructure

## 🎯 Research Objective

The main objective of this research is to investigate how machine learning models can be used to improve prediction and decision-making in smart-grid systems.

The work aims to support more efficient and reliable energy systems for future smart cities.
